### 5. Composition & Layout
The composition centers the human subject while using text to balance the negative space.

*   **Character Position:** The character is centered and cropped from the waist up, dominating the left and central-left portions of the frame. He is looking directly at the viewer.
*   **Product Placement:** The product (boot) is the *primary* focal point within the character's hands. He holds it with both hands in a direct "presenting" pose. It is perfectly horizontal and occupies the center-foreground. The laces are prominent.
*   **Negative Space:** The right side of the frame (approx. 40%) is dedicated entirely to negative background space, which is then filled by the multi-line quote. This provides excellent balance.
*   **Rule of Thirds:** The character's face is positioned on the upper-left vertical intersection. The main body of the product (the boot) is centered horizontally in the frame, slightly below the center horizontal intersection, in the foreground.

*   **Lighting:** Soft, studio-style three-point lighting that is even and warm. It highlights the details of the leather and the character’s glasses without harsh shadows.
*   **Contrast Ratio:** Medium-low overall contrast. The text contrasts cleanly with the background. The character's dark vest contrasts sharply with his light shirt and background. The leather boot has high contrast highlights.
